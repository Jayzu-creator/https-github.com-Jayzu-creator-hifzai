# HifzAI Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026

HifzAI is a Quran reading and memorisation companion. Some features require an
account. Payment and AI recitation features are controlled by server configuration
and may not be available in every release.

## Information we process

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address, authentication
credentials, and account identifiers so HifzAI can sign you in. HifzAI's server
stores payment checkout references, plan access dates, and recitation-check usage
against your account. Your quiz result for each ayah (remembered or needs revision)
is saved to your account to show progress and, for eligible plans, generate
personalised revision recommendations and reports. Yoco processes payment
information directly during checkout; HifzAI does not receive or store your full
card details.

## Microphone and recitation transcription

The microphone is used only after you select a Surah and ayah, agree to the
recording notice, and start recording. The app records a short WAV clip (up to 30
seconds) and sends it to the HifzAI server only after you choose to submit it. The
server holds the audio in memory while processing it; HifzAI does not save the
recording to its database or disk. The clip is forwarded to OpenAI to produce an
Arabic transcript. The app displays the transcript and an approximate text
similarity result; it does not provide a reliable Tajweed grade or religious
ruling.

OpenAI processes audio under its own terms and privacy policy. HifzAI does not
control the provider's processing or retention. Do not submit audio unless you are
comfortable with this processing. Transcripts are returned to your device and are
not saved by HifzAI.

## Quran content and network access

The full Uthmani Quran text is bundled in the app and reading or taking the
memorisation quiz does not send Quran text requests over the network. When a
recitation check is submitted, HifzAI's server requests the selected Surah from
the AlQuran Cloud API as a reference. The provider receives the server's network
request information, not the recording or the user's device network request.

## Data requests and account deletion

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or support, contact
**hifzalbusinesss@gmail.com**. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and
the retention requirements of our service providers.

## Children and changes

HifzAI is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.
This policy will be updated before material changes to data practices are released.
